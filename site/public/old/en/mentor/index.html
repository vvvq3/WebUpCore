FAQ

Honest answers to tough questions

How is Mentor different from an AI code reviewer?

A reviewer looks at one MR. Mentor sees a developer’s full history — tasks, prompts, code and scores — and turns recurring mistakes into a growth plan.

Will Mentor replace the team lead?

No. It takes over the routine — blanket review and common mistakes — so the team lead can focus on architecture and people.

Isn’t this just another form of control?

Recommendations go to the developer and are about growth, not punishment. The digital team lead becomes a mentor, not a controller.

How does Mentor teach working with AI?

It reviews prompts and solutions: where context was missing and where AI went off track. The goal is for developers to grow with AI, not hand their work over to it.
