FAQ

Honest answers to tough questions

Can it work without the prompt collector?

Yes. The collector is optional. From the code alone, UpCore detects AI code with over 92% accuracy — on a month of work, and for the grade — on 3 months.

Isn’t this spying on prompts?

Prompts are analyzed for review and training: where context gets lost and how to get results faster. Access is role-based, and on-premise everything stays with you.

How do you calculate efficiency with AI?

The model knows how many hours a task would take manually and compares that with the real time it took with AI — for every MR, with no trackers or reports.

Do we have to wait until statistics pile up?

No. Six months of history is already in your Git — UpCore analyzes it in an hour and immediately shows the picture “before” and “after” AI.
